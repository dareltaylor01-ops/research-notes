## 1. Summary (5 bullets)

1. **The chip-layer margins in the seed list mostly hold, but they are moving.** Nvidia GAAP gross margin was 75.0% for the quarter ended Jul 26, 2026, with Q3 guidance of 74.0% ±50 bp (NVIDIA, https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-second-quarter-fiscal-2027, 2026-08-26). TSMC was 66.4% in Q3'26, down 1.3 pp QoQ because of N2 ramp dilution, with Q4 guidance of 65–67% (TSMC, https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3346, 2026-10-15). ASML was **57.5%**, not 53%, with FY26 guidance of about 56% (ASML, https://investor.asml.com/news-releases/news-release-details/asml-reports-eu11.7-billion-total-net-sales-and-eu5.0-billion-net-income-q3-2026, 2026-10-14).
2. **The rental tier is not merely "near zero": it loses money after financing costs, and its debt is priced as distressed.** CoreWeave Q2'26 GAAP operating margin was −2% (adjusted 5%), with net interest expense of $640M on $2,575M revenue (CoreWeave, https://investors.coreweave.com/news/news-details/2026/CoreWeave-Reports-Strong-Second-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx, 2026-08-11). Its 9.75% 2031 bond yielded 15.97% on Oct 20 (CNBC, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/20/coreweave-debt-janus-henderson-lloyd.html, 2026-10-20). Gartner predicts over 80% of neoclouds will cease independent operations by 2030 (Gartner, https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-10-14-gartner-says-over-us-dollars-three-and-a-half-trillion-of-artificial-intelligence-infrastructure-spending-could-be-exposed-to-impairment-by-the-end-of-2030, 2026-10-14).
3. **Constraints are stacked, not a single bottleneck.** TSMC's CEO said demand is "still far above our capability today to supply," described "a little bit shortage on the back end," and called US data-center power "so far so good" (TSMC Q3'26 call transcript, https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/15/tsmc-tsm-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/, 2026-10-15). ASML expects that its planned 2027 and 2028 supply "will not fully meet the demand" (ASML, 2026-10-14, URL above). Goldman lists power, transmission, interconnection, transformers, permitting, labor and local opposition (Goldman Sachs, https://www.goldmansachs.com/what-we-do/investment-banking/insights/articles/navigating-a-system-of-ai-bottlenecks, 2026-10-19).
4. **Two of the three AI Stack seed numbers needed correction.** "272 GW announced globally" is really Enverus's estimate of **US Lower-48 potential** capacity on 136,000 buildable acres at an assumed 2.0 MW/acre (Enverus, https://www.enverus.com/newsroom/data-center-sites-unseen-2026-parcel-update/, 2026-04-22). "Largest operating campuses under 1 GW" no longer holds: Epoch estimates Anthropic-Amazon New Carlisle at about 1,037 MW of current IT power, with an estimate band of roughly ±1.4x (Epoch AI, https://epoch.ai/graphs/largest-ai-data-centers-by-power-capacity, updated 2026-10-26).
5. **The demand-mix seed is verified, and McKinsey adds a counterpoint.** McKinsey says inference will be about 60% of AI demand by 2030, against about 40% for training. The same piece argues that durable value may shift from today's scarcity layers to the layers that improve system economics: memory, interconnect, cooling and power delivery (McKinsey, https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/semiconductors/our-insights/scaling-ai-inference, 2026-10-05).

## 2. What moved since last run

This is the first run, so there is no prior run to compare against. The baseline is SC's AI Stack publish date (2026-05-01). Items dated after that:

- **TSMC's margin turned down.** Q2'26 was 67.7% and Q3'26 was 66.4%. Overseas-fab dilution is expected to widen to 3–4% "in the next few years" (TSMC 3Q26 management report, https://investor.tsmc.com/english/quarterly-results/2026/q3, 2026-10-15; call transcript, 2026-10-15).
- **ASML's margin rose,** from 54.0% in Q2'26 to 57.5% in Q3'26. ASML is also "investigating ways to increase capacity for 2028 beyond the 30%" (ASML, 2026-10-14).
- **The neocloud credit selloff hit in October.** CoreWeave bonds fell "below 75 cents," with yields "nearly 16 per cent," and Nvidia "shed more than $300bn in market value over the past week" (FT via Yahoo Finance, https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/investors-dump-data-centre-bonds-132608292.html, 2026-10-15). CoreWeave's 5-year CDS stood at about 1,081 bp (CNBC, 2026-10-20).
- **Vendor financing scaled up.** Nvidia announced partnerships to "mobilize over $500 billion of third-party capital" for AI infrastructure (NVIDIA, 2026-08-26). Bloomberg reports chip-backed debt is "on track to become a trillion-dollar market" (Bloomberg via Yahoo Finance, https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/blackstone-bet-chips-drove-debt-111500562.html, 2026-10-23).
- **GPU rental prices firmed.** The H100 SXM settled index stood at $3.03/GPU-hr on Oct 25, +11.8% month-to-date, with a 30-day range of $2.49–$3.21 (Ornn OCPI-H100, https://data.ornn.com/markets/h100-sxm, 2026-10-25). This is a single source, so treat it as indicative.
- **Gartner put a size on the revenue gap.** It puts $8.5T of required revenue through 2030 against no more than $3.6T forecast AI revenue, with over $3.5T of spending exposed to impairment by end-2030 (Gartner, 2026-10-14).

## 3. Gaps by flag type

### Flag 1: Stale snapshot / market moved
- **G1 (M) Margin math.** ASML's 53% is outdated: Q3'26 was 57.5% and the FY26 guide is about 56% (ASML, 2026-10-14). TSMC is trending down from 67.7% to 66.4% (TSMC, 2026-10-15). Nvidia is flat at 75.0% with a 74.0% Q3 guide (NVIDIA, 2026-08-26). The "75/66/53" set also appears in a third-party piece (Chris Zeoli, Data Gravity, https://www.datagravity.dev/p/who-captures-value-in-ai-infrastructure, date not shown in fetch). Whether SC's deck uses these exact figures is **UNVERIFIED (attribution)**.
- **G2 (H) Rental and neocloud tier.** SC's Sep 8 piece presents neoclouds as a structural category "forecast to approach $400B in revenue by 2031" with GPU-hours "50-70% below hyperscaler list prices" (SC, https://research.socialcapital.com/p/neoclouds, 2026-09-08). Since then, CoreWeave's GAAP operating margin has been −2% and its bonds have been priced as distressed (CoreWeave, 2026-08-11; CNBC, 2026-10-20; FT/Yahoo, 2026-10-15). Gartner expects over 80% of neoclouds to lose independence by 2030 (Gartner, 2026-10-14). The Economist reports that only two of ten listed neoclouds are profitable and that combined borrowings are $100bn, about 3x revenue (Economist via Hindustan Times, https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/the-ai-industry-s-gigantic-debts-are-making-investors-nervous-101792402325349.html, 2026-10-19).
- **G3 (M) "Largest operating campuses under 1 GW".** This is overtaken: New Carlisle is about 1,037 MW of current IT power and Colossus 2 about 946 MW. These are Epoch estimates with roughly ±1.4x error (Epoch AI, updated 2026-10-26).

### Flag 2: Sourcing discrepancy
- **G4 (M) "272 GW announced globally".** Enverus's 272 GW is *potential* US Lower-48 capacity tied to developer-held acreage, not announced and not global (Enverus, 2026-04-22). Other public trackers give much lower "announced" totals, but we have not vetted them; they are logged as leads only.
- **G5 (L) "Assembly/rental near zero".** Measures disagree. CoreWeave's GAAP operating margin is −2% while its adjusted operating margin is +5% (CoreWeave, 2026-08-11). Data Gravity cites Dell at 11% operating margin and Supermicro at 8% gross margin (Data Gravity, date not shown). The Dell and Supermicro figures are **UNVERIFIED** because we did not check their filings this run.

### Flag 3: Missing mechanism
- **G6 (M) Who funds the chip margin.** SC's public excerpt places value at chokepoints such as ASML, TSMC and CUDA (SC, 2026-05-01) but does not describe the financing loop that sustains demand. Nvidia is mobilizing over $500B of third-party capital (NVIDIA, 2026-08-26) and backstopping up to about $300bn of customer debt (Economist via HT, 2026-10-19). Of AI-to-AI deals in 2021–2025, 46.4% by value also involved supplier-customer relationships (BIS Bulletin 137, https://www.bis.org/publications/bulletin-137-circular-relationships-among-ai-firms, 2026-10-01). Part of chip-layer margin durability is therefore a credit question. This overlaps with Private Credit and should be carried to that run.
- **G7 (M) Interest cost is what turns rental economics negative.** CoreWeave's net interest of $640M against a $49M operating loss shows that financing, not operations, drives the net loss (CoreWeave, 2026-08-11). SC's Sep 8 teaser mentions "borrowing costs that can consume its operating profit," but the public text gives no figures (SC, 2026-09-08). The full deck is paywalled, so we cannot tell whether it covers this.

### Flag 4: Missing counter-evidence
- **G8 (M) "Intelligence is becoming free" (model price down 1,500x in six years, SC 2026-05-01).** Per-token prices may fall while total cost to serve rises. About 20% of respondents say AI operating costs, including tokens, constrain use (McKinsey State of AI 2026, https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai, 2026-08-25). Server makers told customers that prices would rise by more than 15% in many cases (Bloomberg via Yahoo, 2026-10-23). The H100 rental index is up 11.8% in October (Ornn, 2026-10-25). The 1,500x figure itself is SC's; we have not traced its source (**UNVERIFIED**).
- **G9 (M) Fixed fulcrum view.** McKinsey argues that durable value "may accrue instead to the layers that measurably improve system economics," such as memory, interconnect, cooling and power delivery (McKinsey, 2026-10-05). This cuts against a static list of fulcrum assets.

### Flag 5: Single-constraint framing
- **G10 (H) "Why power is the binding constraint"** (SC neocloud teaser, 2026-09-08). Evidence points to stacked constraints:
  - wafers: demand is "far above our capability today to supply" (TSMC call, 2026-10-15)
  - back-end packaging: "a little bit shortage," with GlobalFoundries supplying interposers (TSMC call, 2026-10-15)
  - lithography: 2027–2028 supply "will not fully meet the demand" (ASML, 2026-10-14)
  - grid: a US interconnection queue of about 2,600 GW and a median of about 5 years from application to operation
  - equipment: transformer lead times of about 3–5 years
  - local opposition: 86 projects canceled or delayed since Jan 2026, 76 of them tied to local opposition (Goldman, 2026-10-19)

  TSMC's own check of US power was "so far so good" (TSMC call, 2026-10-15), so power does not bind first everywhere.
